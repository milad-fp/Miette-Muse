We believe the best pastries begin long before the first bite—with cultured butter, patient doughs, and recipes worth keeping.

Miette & Muse began around a tiny kitchen table and a stubborn devotion to beautiful things. Today, our bakers fold, pipe, glaze, and finish every piece by hand. French at heart, joyfully modern in spirit, always made for sharing.
